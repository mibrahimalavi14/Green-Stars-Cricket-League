🤔 Extra kya hota hai? — jo run ball ko bat se nahi mare, unhe Extra kehte hain.
1 Wides — ball batsman ki pahunch se door chali gayi. Jo bhi runs bhaage (wide ke) wo yahan daalein. Example: 2 wides = 2
2 No Balls — bowler ne overstep ki ya full toss waist se upar. 1 extra run + jo bhi batsman ne maar rakhe. Example: 1 no-ball = 1
3 Byes — ball batsman ko nahi lagi, wicketkeeper bhi na pakar saka, aur runs bhaag gaye. Example: 2 byes = 2
4 Leg Byes — ball batsman ki leg/body se lagi (bat se nahi) aur runs bhaag gaye. Example: 1 leg bye = 1

⚡ Extras Kaise daalein — Examples

🏏 No-ball + batsman 4 maare:
Batsman Runs = 4 | No Balls = 1
🏏 Wide ball (batsman ne kuch nahi kiya):
Kisi batsman ko kuch nahi | Wides = 1
🏏 Wide + 2 extra runs bhaage (total 3 wide runs):
Kisi batsman ko kuch nahi | Wides = 3
🏏 No-ball + batsman 0:
Batsman Runs = 0 | No Balls = 1
🏏 Ball misses everything, 2 byes:
Batsman Runs = 0 | Byes = 2
🏏 Ball hits pad, 1 leg bye:
Batsman Runs = 0 | Leg Byes = 1

⚠️ Important Notes

✅ Balls Faced (bf) — max 30 hai (5 overs). Wides/no-balls yahan nahi ginate.
✅ Balls Bowled (bb) — koi limit nahi. Wides/no-balls bhi yahan shamil hain.
✅ Bowling Runs (Runs) — khali chhoro to auto-calculate ho jayega (balls proportion se). Apni marzi se bhi daal sakte ho.
✅ Wickets — "Out" checkbox se count hote hain, bowling Wkts column se nahi.
✅ Neutral Fielders — agar doosri team ka player fielding kare to "Starter Phase" section use karein.
🔄 Edit — galti ho jaye to dubara "Edit Result" karke change kar sakte hain.

❓ Common Mistakes

❌ Bhoolna: Out checkbox ke baad dismissal select karna
✅ Hamesha Out check karein AUR dismissal select karein
❌ Bhoolna: dono teams ke players ka data daalna
✅ Pehle Team 1 table bharein, phir Team 2 table
❌ Bhoolna: Toss Winner select karna
✅ Toss ke baghair result calculate nahi ho ga
